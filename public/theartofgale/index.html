About the Artist

“In my work, I look for the quiet friction between internal emotion and distorted memory.”

Olivia Gale Gerochi — also known as Gale — is a contemporary painter and visual artist based in Iloilo City, Philippines. Working primarily in oil, mixed media, and fluid watercolor, her studio practice explores the anatomy of feeling, personal mythology, and the surreal atmospheres of memory.

Gale treats every canvas as an emotional excavation. Her work balances delicate, intentional drawing with visceral, layered mark-making, capturing fleeting states of longing, evanescense, and internal change. Influenced by literary romanticism and the weight of unspoken words, her paintings navigate the boundary between conscious thought and dream logic.

Gale's studio practice explores vulnerability, personal myth, and the pull of alternate realities.
